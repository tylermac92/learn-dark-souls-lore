# Dark Souls Lore Resources

## Knowledge

- In-game text (opening cinematics, NPC dialogue, item descriptions)
  The only canonical source. The series tells its story mostly through item descriptions. Use for: every factual claim; quote it directly.
- [Fextralife Dark Souls Wiki: Lore](https://darksouls.wiki.fextralife.com/Lore)
  Full transcription of the DS1 opening narration plus location/character lore and item text. Use for: checking exact in-game wording. Treat its interpretations as fan theory.
- [Fextralife Dark Souls 2 Wiki: Lore](https://darksouls2.wiki.fextralife.com/lore) and the Dark Souls 3 Fextralife wiki
  Same role for DS2 and DS3.
- [Dark Souls wikidot](https://darksouls.wikidot.com/)
  Older wiki with clean item and dialogue transcriptions (e.g. Humanity, Kaathe). Read-only now, but text is reliable. Use for: verbatim DS1 text.
- [Dark Souls Fandom Wiki](https://darksouls.fandom.com/wiki/The_First_Flame)
  Character and concept pages that cite item text. Some pages carry "lacks sources" warnings: verify claims against quoted text.
- [Walantus, "Dark Souls" narrative review, GDC 2017](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2017/GameNarrativeReview/Calvin_Walantus_UCSC_GDC_Narrative.pdf)
  Analysis of how DS1 delivers its story. Use for: why the lore is fragmentary, and the role of the opening cinematic.
- [VaatiVidya (YouTube)](https://www.youtube.com/@VaatiVidya)
  The best-known lore channel; "Prepare to Cry" series for DS1, separate series for DS2 and DS3. Use for: narrated deep dives after a lesson. Mixes evidence with interpretation.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/DarkSoulsLore](https://www.reddit.com/r/DarkSoulsLore/)
  Dedicated lore subreddit. Use for: testing an interpretation, asking "what's the evidence for X?".
- [r/darksouls](https://www.reddit.com/r/darksouls/)
  Larger general community. Use for: casual lore talk.

## Gaps

- Specific VaatiVidya video URLs not yet verified (lessons link a YouTube search instead).
- No vetted source yet for DS2's connection to DS1 or for DS3's ending lore.
