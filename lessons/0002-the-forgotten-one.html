Lesson 2 · Era II · About 10 minutes

The Forgotten One

The opening narration skips over one of the four. This lesson is about what that one took, and why it makes you what you are.

Your win today: explain why humans in Dark Souls belong to the Dark, citing two pieces of in-game evidence. You'll also be able to say who told you, which matters more than it sounds.

Warm-up

From Lesson 1. No peeking.

The gap in the story

In Lesson 1, four figures found Lord Souls, but only three fought the dragons. The narration calls the fourth, the Furtive Pygmy, “so easily forgotten” and never mentions them again. It never says what the Pygmy found in the flame.

The answer comes from someone else, much later, in a place most players reach only once: the Abyss beneath New Londo, after the Four Kings.

Evidence 1 · Kaathe's account

Darkstalker Kaathe is one of the two primordial serpents. If you speak to him while carrying the Lordvessel, he tells you this:

After the advent of fire, the ancient lords found the three souls. But your progenitor found a fourth, unique soul. The Dark Soul.

Your ancestor claimed the Dark Soul and waited for Fire to subside. … Thus began the age of men, the Age of Dark.

Darkstalker Kaathe, the Abyss (Lordvessel in inventory)
Dialogue as transcribed on the Dark Souls wikidot and Fextralife pages for Kaathe.

Three things in four sentences:

  • Three souls plus one. The Lord Souls are three. The fourth soul is different in kind: the Dark Soul.
  • “Your progenitor.” Kaathe is talking to you. The Pygmy is the ancestor of humans.
  • The Age of Dark is the age of men. When the fire fades, the narration says “only Dark will remain.” Kaathe adds that this is the age humans were always meant to inherit.

Evidence 2 · The Humanity item

You picked up dozens of these. Read the description again with Kaathe's words in mind:

Rare tiny black sprite found on corpses. Use to gain 1 humanity and restore a large amount of HP.

This black sprite is called humanity, but little is known about its true nature. If the soul is the source of all life, then what distinguishes the humanity we hold within ourselves?

Humanity, item description (DS1)
Item text from the Dark Souls wikidot Humanity page.

The item won't say outright what humanity is. But it is black, every human carries it, and it is explicitly something other than a soul. Put that next to “your ancestor claimed the Dark Soul” and you get the standard reading: humanity is a fragment of the Dark Soul, passed down from the Pygmy to every human.

Text vs. reading

Notice the difference in strength. “Your progenitor found … the Dark Soul” is in-game text. “Humanity is a piece of the Dark Soul” is a reading: almost every lore source agrees with it, but you build it by putting two clues together. Dark Souls lore works like this throughout, and from now on this course will tell you which kind of claim you're looking at.

Who's talking?

Kaathe has a reason to tell you all this. Right after the history, he turns it into an accusation:

Lord Gwyn trembled at the Dark. Clinging to his Age of Fire, and in dire fear of humans, … By sacrificing himself to link the fire, and commanding his children to shepherd the humans, Gwyn has blurred your past, to prevent the birth of the Dark Lord.

Darkstalker Kaathe, same conversation

Kaathe wants you to kill Gwyn and let the fire die. His history might be true, but it is told by someone who wants something from you. That doesn't make him a liar. It means his account is testimony, not narration. In Lesson 3 you'll meet the other serpent, who wants the opposite, and hear Gwyn's side.

The shape to remember

Three Lord Souls + one Dark Soul. The Pygmy took the Dark Soul → humans descend from the Pygmy → humanity is that dark inheritance → the Age of Dark is the age of men. Gwyn, according to Kaathe, linked the fire to stop it.
