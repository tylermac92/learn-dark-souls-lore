Reference · canonical terms for this course

Lore Glossary

The words this course uses, and the one meaning each carries. A term is added only after you've used it correctly in a quiz.

Ages

Age of Ancients
The world before fire: fog, gray crags, Archtrees and Everlasting Dragons, without change or death.
Age of Fire
The age begun when the Lords defeated the dragons, lit by the First Flame and now fading.

Fire and souls

First Flame
The fire that appeared in the Age of Ancients and created Disparity.Avoid: “the bonfire” (bonfires are a different thing)
Disparity
The opposites the First Flame brought: heat and cold, life and death, light and dark.
Lord Soul
One of the souls of great power found in the First Flame by Nito, the Witch of Izalith and Gwyn.
Dark Soul
The fourth, unique soul, found by the Furtive Pygmy, the ancestor of humans.Avoid: calling it a fourth Lord Soul. Kaathe sets it apart from “the three souls.”
Linking the Fire
A Lord giving himself to the First Flame as fuel to prolong the Age of Fire, as Gwyn did.Avoid: “relighting” or “rekindling” the fire; those are bonfire terms.
Lord of Cinder
One who has linked the fire and burned as its fuel; Gwyn's title at the Kiln.
Lordvessel
The vessel given to the Undead destined to succeed Gwyn; filled with Lord Souls, it opens the way to the Kiln.
Humanity
The black sprite every human carries, explicitly not a soul; read as a fragment of the Dark Soul.

Ages, continued

Age of Dark
The age that follows when the fire subsides; per Kaathe, the age of men.

Reading the lore

Text
A claim the game makes in its own words: narration, dialogue or item description.
Reading
An interpretation built by combining pieces of text.Avoid: “theory” for well-supported readings; save it for speculation.
Testimony
Text spoken by a character with a stake in what you believe, such as Kaathe.

The Undead

Darksign
The brand of an accursed Undead; those who bear it are reborn after death.
