The Full Arc

The story from the Age of Ancients to the end of Dark Souls III, compressed. One era is added each time a lesson covers it.

Era I · The Age of Ancients to the Age of Fire

Fog → Fire → Four → War → Fading

  1. Age of Ancients. Unformed world, fog, gray crags, Archtrees, Everlasting Dragons. No change, no death.
  2. The First Flame brings disparity: heat/cold, life/death, light/dark.
  3. From the dark, four find Lord Souls in the flame: Nito (first of the dead), the Witch of Izalith (with the Daughters of Chaos), Gwyn (Lord of Sunlight, with his knights), the Furtive Pygmy (“so easily forgotten”).
  4. War on the dragons. Gwyn: lightning bolts. Witches: firestorms. Nito: miasma of death and disease. Seath the Scaleless betrays the dragons. The Pygmy has no recorded part.
  5. Age of Fire begins, then fades to embers. The Undead bear the Darksign and are locked away in the north (the Undead Asylum).

Source: DS1 opening narration · Lesson 1

Era II · The Dark Soul and humanity

Three Lord Souls + one Dark Soul

  1. The fourth soul. The ancient lords found three souls; the Furtive Pygmy found a fourth, unique one: the Dark Soul. (Kaathe, text)
  2. Humans descend from the Pygmy. Kaathe calls the Pygmy “your progenitor.” (text)
  3. Humanity is the Dark Soul's inheritance. A black sprite every human carries, explicitly not a soul. (Humanity item, text, plus the standard reading)
  4. The Age of Dark is the age of men. When the fire subsides, the Dark that remains belongs to humans. (Kaathe, text)
  5. Gwyn resists. Fearing the Dark and humans, Gwyn links the fire with himself as the sacrifice and has his children shepherd humans. (Kaathe, testimony with an agenda)

Sources: Kaathe's Abyss dialogue, Humanity item · Lesson 2

Era III · Dark Souls: Lordran

Gwyn burns; two serpents; one choice

  1. Gwyn links the fire. He bequeaths most of his power to the Gods and burns as cinder for the First Flame, becoming the Lord of Cinder. (Soul of Gwyn, text; agrees with Kaathe)
  2. Frampt, Gwyn's friend, says your fate is to succeed Gwyn: link the fire, cast away the Dark, undo the Undead curse. (testimony)
  3. The Lordvessel, from Anor Londo, opens the final door once filled with souls equal to Gwyn's: Nito, the Witch of Izalith, the Four Kings, Seath. (text; shards as Gwyn's soul = reading)
  4. Kaathe wants the same souls collected, but for you to become the Dark Lord. (testimony)
  5. The Kiln. After Gwyn falls: light the bonfire = Linking the Fire; walk away = the Dark Lord. (gameplay)

Sources: Soul of Gwyn, Lordvessel, Lord Soul shards, Frampt's dialogue · Lesson 3
