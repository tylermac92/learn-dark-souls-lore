# Mission: Dark Souls Lore (DS1–DS3)

## Why
Curiosity. The user has finished all three games and knows the broad strokes (Gwyn, the First Flame, linking the fire, Hollows), and wants the whole story to finally hang together.

## Success looks like
- Narrate the full arc, from the Age of Ancients through the ending of Dark Souls III, in order, without notes.

## Constraints
- Short, frequent lessons (~10 minutes), each with a quiz and recall of earlier material.
- No spoiler concerns: endings and all DLC are in scope.

## Out of scope
- Demon's Souls, Bloodborne, Sekiro, Elden Ring (unless the user asks).
- Gameplay, builds, boss strategy.
